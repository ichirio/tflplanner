# tflplanner: a Shiny study manager for clinical TFLs

The GUI over tflspec (the Excel specifications, ARD and plan engine) and
rtfreporter (the RTF renderer).

## Start here

[`setup_tflplanner()`](https://ichirio.github.io/tflplanner/reference/setup_tflplanner.md)
once: where the app keeps its settings and where new study folders go
(its home, under
[`tools::R_user_dir()`](https://rdrr.io/r/tools/userdir.html) unless you
choose another). Then
[`run_app()`](https://ichirio.github.io/tflplanner/reference/run_app.md)
opens the app.
[`create_sample_study()`](https://ichirio.github.io/tflplanner/reference/create_sample_study.md)
makes a study to try it on: tflspec's oncology sample data with tables,
a listing and figures, ready to run.
